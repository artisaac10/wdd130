About us - Green Bay Rafters

The Green Bay Rafters goal and mission is to provide all customers with fun and engaging white water rafting experinces within their local community in Green Bay. We are all about providing a customer first experience that will put a smile on anyones face guaranteed!

History

Green Bay Rafters has been open and locally running in Green Bay since 1959. Established first on the famous Fox River Trail, Green Bay Rafters now has tours and rafting oppurtunities throughout Green Bay and the rest of Northeast Wisconsin!
