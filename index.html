Wisconsin

Wisconsin is a state in the Midwest of America. It borders Canada along with several of the Great Lakes. Known primarily for dairy farming, Wisconsin is commonly known for its cheese, beer, and Midwestern hospitality. In church history Wisconsin borders Nauvoo, Illinois and much of the wood used in the construction of the Nauvoo temple came from the state.
